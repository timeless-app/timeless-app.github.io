Download Timeless.

Install in a minute. Pro features unlock with a licence key.

Download Timeless-Setup.exe

Version 2.3.0 · about 80 MB · 64-bit Windows 10 or 11

Install in four steps.

1

Open the file

Open Timeless-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.

2

If Windows warns you

“Windows protected your PC” is normal for new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway.

3

Install

Click through the installer. Timeless adds a Desktop and Start-menu shortcut.

4

Connect the TV

Plug in the projector or TV, open Timeless and click Open viewer. The countdown goes full screen there.

Unlock Pro

After you buy, your key is on the thank-you page and in your email. In Timeless open Help → Timeless Pro…, paste it and click Activate. Internet is needed once, then Timeless works offline.

See plans · Lost your key?

Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A second screen, TV or projector for the audience view. For the phone remote, the phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi. No internet needed to run events.

What’s new in 2.3.

  • Timers stay accurate even if the computer’s clock changes, and catch up correctly after sleep.
  • Crash recovery: reopen Timeless and pick up the event exactly where it was.
  • Phone remote: a separate speaker-view link that can’t control the timer, “Disconnect all” and a connected-phones count.
  • Timeless Pro: Individual, Team and Event Pass licences, and “Show my key”.
