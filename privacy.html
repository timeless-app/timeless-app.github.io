Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Short version: Timeless does not track you. Your schedules, settings and logos stay on your computer.

What the app sends

Only when you activate or remove a Pro licence, and occasionally to check it is still valid, Timeless contacts Freemius with: your licence key, an anonymous ID for your computer, your computer’s name, and the Timeless version. Nothing else is sent. There are no analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

Phone remote

The phone remote works only on your local Wi-Fi, directly between your phone and your laptop. Nothing passes through our servers.

When you buy

Freemius processes your payment and keeps your name, email and order details to provide your licence, receipts and support. See the Freemius privacy policy for details.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no tracking scripts.

Your rights

You can ask us to see or delete the details we hold about you. Contact us using the link on our website.
