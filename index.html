Help

Quick answers. Still stuck? Email us.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Yes. Windows shows this for new apps from small makers until it gets to know them. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I get my licence key after paying?

It’s shown on the thank-you page and emailed to you straight away. In Timeless, open Help → Timeless Pro…, paste the key and click Activate. You need internet once; after that Timeless works offline.

I lost my licence key
  1. Timeless is still activated on your computer? Open Help → Timeless Pro… → Show my key and copy it.
  2. Otherwise: open the customer portal, sign in with the email you paid with, and you’ll see your key and receipts.
  3. Still nothing? Email us from the address you paid with and we’ll send it again.
Moving Timeless to a new computer

On the old computer: Help → Timeless Pro… → Remove from this computer. Then activate on the new one. Lost or broken laptop? Email us and we’ll free it for you.

Does it need the internet?

No. The timer, schedules and audience screen all work offline. The phone remote uses your local Wi-Fi only. Internet is needed just once, to activate Pro.

How does the Event Pass work?

It unlocks every Pro feature on one computer for 7 days from purchase, so buy it close to your event. If it ends while a timer is running, Timeless finishes the session first.

What’s your refund policy?

If Timeless Pro isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of purchase. See the refund policy.
